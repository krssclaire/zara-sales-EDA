# Zara Sales - Exploration Data Analysis (EDA)

## Project Overview
This project focuses on an exploratory data analysis (EDA) of Zara sales data with the goal of extracting **business-relevant insights** related to seasonality, product characteristics and sourcing.  
Insights are presented through an interactive dashboard designed to support data-driven decision making.

Dataset source: [Kaggle - Maryam Idrissi](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/marixe/zara-sales-for-eda?resource=download)  
Tools: **Excel** | **PowerQuery** | **Power BI**

## Business Context
Zara operates in the fast fashion industry, where:
- product life cycles are short
- demand is highly seasonal
- sourcing decisions have a strong impact on costs and availability  

Understanding sales patterns across seasons, materials and product categories is crucial for optimizing inventory, sourcing and pricing strategies.

## Objectives
The main objectives of this project are:
- Analyze the impact of **seasonality** on sales performance  
- Identify top-performing **materials** and product characteristics  
- Explore differences between **Woman** and **Man** product sections  
- Assess the distribution of sales by **country of origin**  

## Analysis & Key Insights
### Seasonality
- Autumn and Winter show the highest sales volumes.
- Seasonal products consistently outperform non-seasonal ones.  

**Business insight:**  
Seasonality plays a key role in driving demand, suggesting the need for early planning and inventory optimization for peak seasons.

### Materials
- Cotton, Wool and Wool Blend are the most sold materials.
- Materials such as Silk and Satin show marginal demand.

**Business insight:**  
Sourcing strategies should prioritize high-rotation materials to reduce overproduction risk.

### Product Sections
- The Woman section generates higher sales volumes.
- The Man section shows higher average prices.

**Key takeaway:**  
*Woman drives volume, Man drives margin.*

### Country of Origin
- Sales are concentrated in a limited number of sourcing countries, mainly in Asia and Europe.

**Business insight:**  
This concentration may represent a potential supply chain risk and an opportunity for diversification.

## Dashboard
The final Power BI dashboard allows interactive exploration of:
- sales by season and seasonality  
- materials and product sections  
- geographic distribution of product origin  

> Note: The PDF report includes a static version of the dashboard. Interactive elements such as the map and heatmap are fully available in Power BI.


## Limitations & Next Steps
- The dataset does not include cost or margin data.
- No time-series forecasting was performed.

Possible future extensions:
- profitability analysis by category  
- price sensitivity analysis  
- demand forecasting by season
